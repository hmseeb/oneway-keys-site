What We Do

Professional Key & Locksmith Services

From simple key copies to advanced transponder programming, ONEWAY Keys has you covered with expert craftsmanship and top-tier equipment.

🔑

Key Duplication

Precision-cut copies of residential, commercial, and padlock keys in minutes. We carry a wide range of blanks to fit nearly any keyway.

🚗

Automotive Keys & Fobs

Lost your car key or need a spare? We program transponder keys, smart keys, and key fobs for most domestic and foreign vehicle makes and models.

Residential Locksmith

Lock installation, rekeying, and repair for your home. Upgrade to high-security deadbolts and protect your family with peace of mind.

Commercial Lock Services

Master key systems, high-security locks, and access control solutions designed to keep your business, employees, and assets secure.

Emergency Lockout Service

Lock Repair & Maintenance

Sticky, worn, or damaged locks repaired by skilled technicians. We restore proper function and extend the life of your existing hardware.
